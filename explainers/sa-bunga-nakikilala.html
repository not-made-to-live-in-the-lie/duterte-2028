02 · Isang trivia sa hardin · A Filipino botanical opening

A surprising tree, a surprising fruit — and Christ's test made visible.

The variegated guava (Psidium guajava 'Variegata'), known to ornamental growers as the Honeymoon Guava, is a striking tropical cultivar of our familiar bayabas. Its leaves do not wear the uniform green of an ordinary guava — they are marbled with creamy white, light green, and deep green stripes, no two leaves alike.

And here is what surprises new gardeners: the fruit is variegated too. The skin carries yellow and green stripes. The flesh inside flushes pink. The tree wears its identity on every leaf, and the fruit confirms what the leaves already said.

The above and the below are of the same nature. The visible above ground continues into what comes from it.

Christ's test is the same shape. He gave us a way of looking that works on trees, and on those who would lead us: by their fruits ye shall know them. The leaves tell us what the tree is. The fruit confirms it. This brief is one attempt to read both, plainly, on the public record of a candidate for the 2028 presidency.

The test, as Christ Himself gave it.

Beware of false prophets, which come to you in sheep's clothing, but inwardly they are ravening wolves. Ye shall know them by their fruits. Do men gather grapes of thorns, or figs of thistles? Even so every good tree bringeth forth good fruit; but a corrupt tree bringeth forth evil fruit. A good tree cannot bring forth evil fruit, neither can a corrupt tree bring forth good fruit. Every tree that bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down, and cast into the fire. Wherefore by their fruits ye shall know them.
Matthew 7:15–20 · KJV
Mag-ingat kayo sa mga bulaang propeta na lumalapit sa inyo na may pananamit-tupa, ngunit sa loob ay mga lobong gutom. Sa kanilang mga bunga ay makikilala ninyo sila. Mamimitas ba ng mga ubas sa mga tinik, o ng mga igos sa mga dawag? Gayon din, bawat mabuting punò ay nagbubunga ng mabuting bunga; ngunit ang punò na masama ay nagbubunga ng masamang bunga.

This is the test. The rest of this brief is its application — to one candidate, to one public record, plainly, with the receipts on the table.

04 · Methodology · How we read, and how we do not judge

Christ's test is for our discernment — not our condemnation.

  • This brief is not a sermon. It does not pronounce on anyone's salvation.
  • It applies a political test that Christ gave us for evaluating those who would lead.
  • Every factual claim carries a numbered citation linked to a live source on the References slide.

Two honesty guards that recur in this brief.

  • On AMLC findings: AMLC reports a transaction's existence, not its legal interpretation. Only a court rules on guilt. We cite AMLC findings as evidence of what is on record, not as a verdict.
  • On her conduct & attention: this brief makes no diagnostic claim about Sara Duterte's mental state and no claim about her inner motives. It cites authenticated public conduct and observable scheduling patterns. You discern.

Sources include Rappler, Inquirer, Al Jazeera, GMA, Philstar, BusinessWorld, MindaNews, Bloomberg, CMFR; COA, AMLC, PSA, BSP, OVP; HRW, Amnesty, Civicus, OCCRP, ICHRP, UNICEF, The Lancet; Pulse Asia, OCTA, SWS. Wikipedia is used for chronology only.

Receipt 1 · Ang unang bunga · Matthew 7:16

The first fruit — the children entrusted to her care.

Do men gather grapes of thorns, or figs of thistles?
Matthew 7:16 · KJV

Education stewardship is one of the visible fruits by which Christ's test asks us to measure. Sara Duterte served as Secretary of the Department of Education from June 30, 2022 to July 19, 2024. The record:

  • 0% accomplishment on equipment delivery in 2023 — 12,000 teacher laptops, 7,500 staff laptops, e-learning carts, smart TVs all undelivered.[13]
  • Matatag curriculum rolled out without teacher training; teachers describe rollout as rushed and chaotic.[13][14]
  • Feeding program: only 48% implementation; billions in funds unutilized while children went hungry.[15]
  • Cash envelopes (~₱50,000/month) distributed to undersecretaries, per testimony of former Usec Gloria Mercado.[16]
  • ₱112.5M in DepEd confidential funds cited in the impeachment articles.[3]
  • Filipino students ranked 77th of 81 countries in PISA 2022.[17]

The children we were called to disciple — anak, apo, inaanak — were the ones whose laptops did not arrive, whose feeding program reached fewer than half, whose curriculum was launched without training their teachers. These are the fruits the leaves of two years had to confirm.

Receipt 3 · Ang ikatlong bunga · Matthew 7:18 · Sirach 27:6 (Catholic Bible)

The third fruit — the bank record her own checks left behind.

A good tree cannot bring forth evil fruit, neither can a corrupt tree bring forth good fruit.
Matthew 7:18 · KJV

A. Volume.

  • AMLC: bank accounts of Sara Duterte and her husband Manases Carpio flagged for covered + suspicious transactions totaling ₱6.77 billion from 2006 to 2025.[23]
  • 313 covered + 17 suspicious transactions in her accounts; 317 covered + 16 suspicious in her husband's.

B. SALN mismatch.

  • Duterte declared no cash on hand or bank deposits for six consecutive years (SALN, 2019–2024).[24]
  • AMLC: ₱230.87M in transactions occurred in those same years. Net worth grew from ₱7M (2007) to ₱88.4M (2024) — a ~12× increase across her public-service career.[24]

C. Drug-lord-linked money — verified by AMLC.

  • AMLC confirmed Sara Duterte received ₱14.8M from Samuel "Sammy" Uy and his associates via check transactions, October 2011 to April 2012.[25]
  • Per the affidavit of former Sen. Antonio Trillanes IV (cross-confirmed by AMLC on multiple entries): Sara Duterte received ₱22.32M from Uy across three transactions; the wider Duterte family received ₱181.6M from Uy in 2011–2013.[26]
  • Sammy Uy is the long-alleged drug personality named in Trillanes' sworn affidavit; he is a business partner of Michael Yang (Rodrigo Duterte's former economic adviser).[28]
  • AMLC caveat (cited in full for honesty): the Council can confirm transactions exist in the records of covered institutions but cannot independently verify direct ownership of all accounts.[27]
  • Duterte's response (cited in full for fairness): "Every cent of my money came from a legitimate source."[29]
The contradiction every Christian voter must hold in prayer. HRW and Amnesty estimate ~30,000 Filipinos — overwhelmingly poor — were killed in the drug war under President Rodrigo Duterte, conducted on the rhetorical premise that drug-linked persons forfeit their right to live.[46] The AMLC record indicates that, while that campaign was being justified, ₱181.6M in transactions allegedly attributable to a businessman named in a sworn affidavit as a drug personality appear in the bank records of multiple Duterte-family members.
These findings are evidentiary, not yet a judicial conviction. Weigh them in prayer with the other receipts in this brief. Christ's heart was for the poor who paid the cost of what was done in their name.

Proverbs 29:2 · KJV

The May 11, 2026 Senate coup as a preview.

  • Tito Sotto ousted as Senate President in a surprise election; Alan Peter Cayetano (long-time Duterte ally) installed by a 13-senator Duterte-aligned bloc.[63]
  • Cayetano now presides over Sara Duterte's own impeachment court.[55]

The senators who would govern with her.

  • Sen. Ronald "Bato" dela Rosa — PNP chief and architect of Oplan Tokhang under Rodrigo Duterte; named by the ICC as a co-perpetrator in the drug-war case; named in the House Quadcom report (June 2025) for state-sanctioned killings.[65][66]
  • Sen. Christopher "Bong" Go — long-time Duterte aide; named by the ICC as a co-perpetrator; tied to the Pharmally scandal.[65][66]

  • Anti-Terrorism Act of 2020 — the law under which 227 individuals have been charged and 34 designated "terrorists".[35]

A vote for Sara Duterte in 2028 is not a vote for Sara Duterte alone. It is a vote that places this entire company in authority over the Filipino people. Proverbs 29:2 leaves us only two states: a people who rejoice, or a people who mourn.

The eighth fruit — the children, the climate, and the debt they will pay.

Proverbs 22:6 · KJV

Lamentations 5:7 · KJV
  • Filipino youth climate vulnerability: 26% of Filipino youth name climate as their biggest future problem (UNICEF)[43]; PH is #1 in the World Risk Index and #7 in Germanwatch's Climate Risk Index 2026.[42]
  • Youth unemployment: 11.7% (744,000 unemployed) as of Nov 2025; 13.1% underemployment as of Mar 2026.[45]
  • The sovereign debt your grandchildren will pay: Rodrigo Duterte's term added ₱6.843 trillion in new borrowings (₱5.948T → ₱12.791T, June 2016 → June 2022) — more than the combined borrowings of all previous PH administrations on the modern record.[50][51]
  • Pharmally Pharmaceutical received ~US$175M in overpriced COVID supply contracts to a firm capitalized at ~US$12,000.[52]

Davao itself — the family's own city, the family's own floods.

A test in plain sight: the Duterte family has held the Davao City mayoralty for roughly three decades — Rodrigo Duterte (1988–1998, 2001–2010, 2013–2016), Sara Duterte herself (2010–2013, 2016–2022), and Sebastian "Baste" Duterte (2022–present). Davao City's 1st congressional district is represented by Sara's brother, Rep. Paolo "Pulong" Duterte.

  • Davao City received ~₱51 billion in infrastructure funds in 2020–2022 — the last three years of President Rodrigo Duterte's term — per the official DPWH figure cited at Senate hearings.[71]
  • Of that total, roughly ₱9.12 billion was specifically earmarked for flood-control systems (₱1.4B in 2020, ₱1.192B in 2021, ₱722.5M in 2022, plus subsequent allocations), per the GAA breakdowns reported by Inquirer and GMA.[71]
  • And yet — on May 18, 2026, six days before this brief was published — heavy rains triggered flooding and power interruptions across Davao City, forcing the city government to suspend classes and work the following day. May 2025 brought similar flooding to the same communities.[72]
  • The Independent Commission for Infrastructure (ICI) has reported that several sections of Davao City's newly-built flood-control structures have already sustained damage just months after completion.[73]

The Lamentations verse is not poetry — it is description. The children of this country will pay this debt across the working life now beginning. The billions allocated for flood control in the family's own city did not produce flood-control structures that hold; they did not produce a city that stays dry. The fruit of three decades of governance in their own city is being measured this very week. A presidency that does not break from this pattern lengthens the shadow.

14 · What this brief is not · What Christ asks of us

A letter, not a sermon. A test, not a verdict.

What this brief is not.

  • Not a partisan endorsement of any other candidate.
  • Not a substitute for prayer or for conscience formation in your parish, congregation, or fellowship.
  • Not a claim that Christ commands you to vote against any specific person — Christ commands no such vote, and we do not claim otherwise.
  • Not a pronouncement on anyone's salvation. Salvation belongs to the Lord.
  • It is an attempt to inform, applying a test Christ gave us. You discern.

What this brief asks of you, kapatid.

  • Read the linked sources yourself. The references are at the end of this deck.
  • Pray. Bring this to your conscience and to those you trust in the faith — your priest, your pastor, your Bible-study leader, a trusted catechist.
  • Talk to one fellow Christian voter about this before October 2027 (when COCs are filed).
  • Register. Verify. Vote.
