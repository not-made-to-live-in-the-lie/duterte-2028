Filipino voters · before 2028

Duterte 2028?
Look at the record before you vote.

A lot of public-record material on the drug war came out after most Filipinos last voted — Senate findings, court rulings, AMLC disclosures, the ICC pre-trial chamber, peer-reviewed research. Two short reads walk through it, sourced and verifiable. Whatever you concluded before, take a fresh look at what's now on record. Decide for yourself.

Written for any Filipino who wants the evidence before voting — whether you supported the drug war, opposed it, or simply lived through it. The two reads share one moral commitment: we can share the lament on both sides; we cannot share the impunity.

NIDA · WHO · CDC · BJS · OJP · HRW · Amnesty · ICC · AMLC · peer-reviewed journals · first-tier PH press

Why look again before 2028.

Most of the public record on the drug war became available after the 2022 vote — Senate findings, AMLC disclosures, the ICC pre-trial chamber, peer-reviewed research. These two reads simply compile what is now on record, so Filipino voters considering a Duterte 2028 ballot can weigh it for themselves.

⚠
A note before you open either deck. Both explainers discuss sexual violence, homicide, extrajudicial killings, and the deaths of named individuals. The discussion is general — not just about the drug war — and the content may stir hard memories for any reader who has survived sexual assault, lost a loved one to violence, or carries unresolved grief or anger from a crime, whether recent or from years ago, related to the drug war or not. The work is meant to honor survivors and the dead, not to flatten them into statistics. Please read at your own pace. Each deck closes with support resources — crisis hotlines, legal-aid groups, and survivor-support organizations in the Philippines.
